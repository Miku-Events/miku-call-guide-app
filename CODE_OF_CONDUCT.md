# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome people regardless of experience or background,
assume good faith while evaluating technical claims on evidence, and keep criticism focused on the
work. Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and the boundaries of the private data repository.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized language,
doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets or personal information, sustained disruption, and abuse
of project infrastructure or contributors.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces
where someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Do not include sensitive personal details in a public issue. The maintainer may edit or remove content, lock conversations,
reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect
the community and project.
